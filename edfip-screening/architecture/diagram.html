Staff and customer portal

Odoo — operating platform

System Administration, licences, module packs, CRM, KYC, customer record, home branch, account officer, VSLA, cooperatives, PayGo operations, agency float, staff screens and customer portal.

Does not post the ledger. When money must move, Odoo asks the integration service.

↔
Apps, USSD, partners, payments, OEM

FastAPI — integration service

Same Emeraid environment as Odoo. Separate process. Maps Odoo IDs to Fineract IDs. Checks institution, branch and licence. Posts each financial instruction once. Webhooks, offline sync, receipts.

Not a bank. Does not write Odoo or Fineract tables. Does not sit in front of staff screens.

Money posts only through the integration service ↓

Apache Fineract — core banking

Loans, savings, schedules, balances, general ledger. Internal service — not a staff website. One ledger. Customer accounts live here; the person, branch and officer live in Odoo, linked by one customer number.

Data stores — separate. No shared tables.
